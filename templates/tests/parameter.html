Important modelling changes in v4
• Field SPT N is corrected using the supplied IS 2131 overburden and dilatancy procedure.
• N1 and final N1' are shown separately; final N1' is used for strength correlations.
• Effective overburden is evaluated at SPT depth + 0.30 m; density is 1.85 gm/cc to 10 m and 2.00 gm/cc below 10 m; groundwater is at ground level.
• Drained φ' is soil-specific; N60 has strongest influence for granular soils.
• Drained c' is no longer strongly proportional to N60.
• Su, Cu and UCS form one strongly correlated undrained family.
• UU Phi now follows the specified reported laboratory ranges by soil behaviour.
For a fully saturated UU interpretation, the theoretical total-stress assumption remains φ = 0°.
The generator is for synthetic/testing datasets and does not replace laboratory measurements.
